Our Services

From the first soil test to the moment we hand over your keys, MK Homes delivers every stage of construction with precision, transparency and timeless finish quality.

Pre-Construction Stage

A strong home begins with careful planning — accuracy, safety, compliance and a solid base for execution.

  • Soil investigation & site survey
  • Architectural concept & 3D planning
  • Structural & MEP design
  • Cost estimation, BOQ & approvals

Site Preparation & Foundation

Precise site setup and a strong foundation for a safe, stable and durable structure.

  • Site cleaning, fencing & layout marking
  • Excavation, PCC & anti-termite treatment
  • Footing reinforcement & concreting
  • Backfilling & compaction

Structure Development

Precision engineering at every step to build a strong, durable and future-ready structure.

  • Plinth beam, DPC & column construction
  • Brickwork, lintel beams & sunshades
  • Slab shuttering & reinforcement
  • Roof slab concreting & curing
View Details 03

Core Building Systems

Strong systems behind every wall ensure safety, durability and lasting performance.

  • Masonry, staircase & parapet walls
  • Terrace, balcony & toilet waterproofing
  • Electrical first fix & earthing
  • Plumbing lines with pressure testing

Finishing Foundation

Finishing works that lay the base for the beautiful, functional spaces that make a house a home.

  • Ceiling, internal & external plastering
  • Floor levelling, screed & tile marking
  • Door, window & main door installation
  • False ceiling GI framework & AC provisions

Interior Luxury Finishes

From elegant interiors to striking exteriors, every finish is crafted for luxury, comfort and timeless style.

  • Texture work, cladding & elevation lighting
  • Modular kitchen, wardrobes & TV units
  • Pooja, crockery, vanity & loft storage
  • Wall paneling, mirrors & granite works

Quality Assurance & Handover

The final steps that ensure every detail is perfect and your dream home is ready for a lifetime of happiness.

  • Painting, second fix & glass installation
  • Railings, hardware & deep cleaning
  • Electrical, plumbing & waterproofing tests
  • Client walkthrough & handover of keys
